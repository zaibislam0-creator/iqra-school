Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

IQRA Digital School (iqradigitalschool.com) is a free learning website for students from KG to F.A in Pakistan. This page explains what information we use and why.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We never receive your Google password. In the app you may also choose your class and, optionally, your city and phone number. Your learning progress (such as stars and completed lessons) is stored so it follows you across devices.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, show your name and class, and save your progress. We do not sell your information, show advertisements, or share it with advertisers or other third parties.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Some preferences are stored on your own device in the browser.

Children

Students who are minors should use the site with a parent or teacher. We only ask for the minimum details needed to run the app.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your account data deleted, email us and we will remove it.

Contact

Email: iqradigitalschoolids@gmail.com
